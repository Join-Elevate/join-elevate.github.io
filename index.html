FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Call or text (602) 628-8122 or DM us on Instagram.

Do I need sales experience?
No. We train you on everything: the pitch, handling objections and closing. What we look for is drive, coachability and a strong work ethic. If you do have door-to-door experience, we'd love to talk too.
Is this a summer job or year-round?
Both options exist. Pest control teams run a seasonal summer push, while fiber internet teams sell year-round. We'll talk through which fits your schedule, whether you're a student or looking for something full-time.
What about housing and travel?
Many of our teams sell in markets away from home. Housing and travel details vary by team and market, so your manager will walk you through the specifics, including any costs, during the interview process before you commit to anything.
How does pay work?
Pay is commission-based, so your income is tied to your results. We cover the full pay structure, hours and day-to-day life in detail during your interview calls.
How fast will I hear back?
We aim to reach out within 24 hours of your application. If you want to move even faster, DM ELEVATE to @elevatesales.official on Instagram.
